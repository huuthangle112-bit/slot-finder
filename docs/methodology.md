# PMO Methodology

## Demand Planning

Incoming customer projects create demand for roles, skills, effort, and delivery timelines.

## Supply Planning

Specialists provide available capacity, expertise, and role coverage.

## Gap Analysis

The app compares project demand against available specialist supply to identify feasible and risky assignments.

## Slack / Float

Slack measures how much schedule buffer remains before the deadline after estimated work is completed.

## Utilization

Utilization measures assigned work compared with available capacity.

## Resource Calendar

Specialist leave and availability affect whether a person can be assigned.

## Resource Leveling

When constrained resources are overloaded, the app flags delivery risk and capacity pressure.

## Resource Smoothing

When slack exists, work can be shifted to reduce workload peaks without missing the deadline.

## Risk Monitoring

The app highlights overload, low slack, missing role coverage, missing skill coverage, and bottleneck roles.

## Human-in-the-Loop Decision

The app recommends and explains squad options, but the PMO or operations lead remains responsible for final approval.
